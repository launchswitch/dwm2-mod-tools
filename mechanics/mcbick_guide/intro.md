## Intro to Skills

Skills can be divided into 6 types; Physical, Spell, Dance, Breath, Field, and Normal. Furthmore, most skills will have an attribute associated to them that will affect their effectiveness against a monster's species. Skills are learned by leveling specific monsters, in addition to having the required stats to learn them. So long as you acquire the stats needed to learn a skill you will always learn it upon leveling up, even if you are above the level to learn it. Some skills may be learned by knowing a multitude of other skills. Below is a short introduction of each type of skill.

**Skills - Physical**

These type of skills function similarly to a basic attack, but with an added effect. They use the same damage formula as the FIGHT command, but may have a damage modifier. This type of skill typically requires HP, ATK, and AGL to learn. The damage formula for the basic attack is as follows: [ATK/2 - DEF/4]

**Skills - Spell**

These type of skills have a variety of effects. Learning a variety of spells can help you learn new and powerful skills. Mix and match spells to learn the ultimate skill in the game! This type of skill typically requires MP and INT to learn.

**Skills - Breath**

These type of skills will inflict damage or status ailments. They have some of the easier requirements and are powerful in the early to mid game. This type of skill typically requires HP to learn.

**Skills - Dance**

These type of skills inflict various ailments, with a few healing and defensive ones. They are great skills to learn for the early to mid game, but may be hard to acquire. They can also be used to learn some powerful skills. This type of skill typically requires HP, AGL, and INT to learn.

**Skills - Field**

These type of skills may only be used on the field, and will help you navigate through the world.

**Skills - Normal**

These type of skills have a variety of effects, and have some of the strongest effects in the game. Unlike other skill types, this type of skill has few counters to it. Due to how diverse these skills are you will be required to have a variety of stats to learn them, but they are worth the effort.
