## Item Resistances

There are several items in the game that offer resistance bonuses. You can view these bonuses below. These bonuses will add to a monster's resistance. For example, if a monster has a resistance of 0 to **Blaze** and they were to equip **MagicCape**, their resistance would then be 1. However, you cannot increase a resistance beyond 3, attempting to do so would only waste your monster's limited equipment slot. Also, items play no role when breeding resistances.
