## Personalities

Personalities affect how your monster acts in battle and the likelihood they'll take certain actions and are determined by three major traits, with a fourth hidden one. The fastest way to change a personality is by using PLAN in battle. Each plan boosts one trait by a lot, while reducing another a little. Fight will NOT affect their personality, but they will fight according to their set plan. Each trait also has a bonus effect in combat when high and a negative effect when low. Monsters will change personality more quickly when low level and slower when high level, making it more important to condition them early. Outside of combat the only way to affect personality are Book items that will raise/lower various traits.

- There are four traits that affect personality, Bravery, Caring, Prudence, Motivation
- Using PLAN in combat is the fastest way to affect personality. FIGHT and ITEM have no effect.
- Lower level monsters change personalities more easily, high levels more slowly.
- Each trait also has bonus effects that are more likely to proc when high or low.

| **Trait** | **High Effect** | **Low Effect** | **Boosted** | **Lowered** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bravery** | Chance for critical hit with physical attack | -- | Charge, QuestBK | Caring, Run, HorrorBK |
| **Caring** | Use healing skill with no MP cost | Dodges an attack, but hits ally | Cautious, BeNiceBK | Mixed, CheaterBK |
| **Prudence** | Ignores enemy resistance for status effects | Counters one attack | Mixed, SmartBK | Charge, ComedyBK |
| **Motivation** | 50% damage boost<br>Boosts trait increases | Dodges one enemy attack<br>Boosts trait decreases | Any action other than Command and Run | Command, Run |

**Charge**= +++ Bravery / -Prudence
**Mixed**= +++ Prudence / - Cautious
**Caring**= +++ Cautious / - Bravery
**Run**= --- Bravery / --- Motivation
