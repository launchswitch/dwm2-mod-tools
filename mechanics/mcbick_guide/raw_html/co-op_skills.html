Introduction to Co-Op Skills

What are Co-Op skills? A Co-Op skill, or a cooperative skill, is a skill that is used when two monsters use two specific skills together, in a single turn. An example would be if MonsterX used BlazeMost and MonsterY used Blizzard in the same turn, this would result in the Co-Op skill Blitzer triggering. Each Co-Op skill has its own required skills. The order in which you use these skills does not matter, as those skills will not be used if a Co-Op skill is triggered. The Co-Op skill will not always trigger when the correct skills are used during battle. Every Co-Op skill has its own chance to trigger. There are two main components to successfully trigger a Co-Op skill, these are personality and motivation.

It is important to make sure the monsters using the required skills don't have personalities that clash. You can find a list of personalities that clash here: https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/gbc/197155-dragon-warrior-monsters/faqs/8155. For the most part you can use the same personality for both monsters without having to keep track of clashing personalities. The exceptions to this are LONE WOLF, VAIN, SMUG, SNOBBY, STUBBORN, REBEL, SPOILED, and LAZY. If your monsters have clashing personalities then they will never trigger a Co-Op skill. You can trigger a Co-Op skill with any personality, except LAZY, as long as they don't clash. The odds of triggering a Co-Op skill is unaffected by personality, so you don't have to worry about getting a HOTBLOOD personality for your monsters to maximize your odds.

The most important factor to trigger a Co-Op skill would have to be your monsters' motivation stat. This is an invisible stat in the game that increases or decreases depending on the actions you take during battle. Unfortunately it is impossible to see this stat without a hex editor for the game. It is very easy to manage though. The stat can only go up to 255 and doesn't take long to max. Every monster you catch or hatch starts with a motivation of 0. Each time you choose Charge, Mixed, or Defense during battle, that monster's motivation will go up. Alternatively, each time you manually select your monsters moves using PLAN>Command, your monster's motivation will go down or if you FLEE from battle it will go down. Using FIGHT or an Item in battle won't affect your monster's motivation. As your motivation increases it can trigger one of two effects during battle. If your monster's motivation is low then it has a chance of dodging all physical attacks during any given turn. If it is high then it has a chance to deal +50% extra damage when using any physical, magical, or breath attack. Most importantly though, your motivation stat will significantly increase the chances of triggering a Co-Op skill. If your monsters triggers a Co-Op skill they will not be able to trigger the other two effects. Without a high motivation stat, you can expect to see a trigger rate of as low as 1-10%. With a compatible personality for both monsters and max motivation stats, you can expect to trigger a Co-Op skill 25-50% of the time when using the required skills for it on the same turn.

It is important to know that Co-Op skills are version exclusive. Cobi and Tara each have a set of 10 Co-Op skills they can use. They are divided into offensive and defensive. Cobi has all the offensive Co-Op skills, while Tara has all the defensive Co-Op skills. This could be a deciding factor when determining which game to invest the majority of your time in. Cobi has one of the best skills in the game, GigaBreak which deals 500-700 damage to all enemies! Tara though, has some of the most competitive and broken skills in the game. She is able to restore your entire team's HP, MP, and Status, including death with the Co-Op Skill Festival of Spirits. If you like buffs then you will love her Twilight Trip Co-Op skill which applies TwinHits, Increase, SpeedUp, and Sidestep buffs on your entire team. Combine it with Enlightenment, another Co-Op Skill, to apply the Focus buff on your entire team and gaurantee every attack is a critical next turn! Even with all of Tara's defensive Co-Op skills, it can hardly protect her from Cobi's Ultimate MegaMagic. Ultimate MegaMagic can deal 8000 damage to all enemies if both monsters have 999 MP, which is enough to wipe out any monster team!

List of Co-Op Skills

Cobi Version

1. Alldain = Thordain + Thordain Effect: 500-700 Damage Target: All Enemies Description: MonsterX and MonsterY turned Thordain into Alldain!

2. Blitzer = BlazeMost + Blizzard Effect: 700-900 Damage Target: One Enemy
Description: MonsterX and MonsterY turned BlazeMost and Blizzard into Blitzer!

3. WaveBlast = GigaSlash + TidalWave Effect: 550 Damage, extra damage to water family. Target: One Enemy Description: MonsterX and MonsterY blasted a Tidalwave with shockwaves to make WaveBlast!

4. RageAir = Gigaslash + Vacuum Effect: 550 Damage, extra damage to bird family. Target: One Enemy Description: MonsterX and MonsterY tore tornadoes with shockwaves to loose RageAir!

5. RageQuake = Gigaslash + Rockthrow Effect: 550 Damage, extra damage to plant family. Target: One Enemy Description: MonsterX and MonsterY rocked the ground with shockwaves to loose RageQuake!

6. GigaBreak = GigaSlash + GigaSlash Effect: 500-700 Damage Target: All Enemies Description: MonsterX and MonsterY turned two GigaSlashes into GigaBreak!

7. Martyr = Sacrifice + Kamikaze Effect: Reduce HP to 1 or 0, reduces both user's MP to 0. Target: All Enemies and MonsterX/Y
Description: MonsterX and MonsterY turned two Sacrifices into Martyr!

8. Sky Attack* = HighJump + Geyser Effect: Double Damage Target: One Enemy
Description: MonsterX and MonsterY hurled "Target" high and dropped it!

9. Faster SquallHit* = SquallHit + SpeedUp Effect: Stronger SquallHit, unknown damage calculation(At maxed stats 980-1020 damage). Target: One Enemy Description: MonsterX and MonsterY made SquallHit faster using SpeedUp!

10. Ultimate MegaMagic* = MegaMagic + MPassmost Effect: Stronger MegaMagic (Damage = MonsterX/Y's MP X 4) Target: All Enemies Description: MonsterX and MonsterY let loose their entire magical power! It set off a wicked explosion!

Tara Version

1. Festival of Spirits* = LifeSong + Hustle Effect: Restore All HP, MP, and Status, including death. Target: All Allies Description: MonsterX and MonsterY sang and danced to summon a spirit!

2. Ritual of Death* = Curse + KODance Effect: Inflict Poison, Confusion, Sleep, Paralysis, or Curse status, ignoring resistances. Target: All Enemies Description: MonsterX and MonsterY sang and danced to summon death!

3. Great Change* = Transform + Chance Effect: Stronger BeDragon (999HP, 300MP, EvilSlash, Eerielite, SuckAll) Target: MonsterX/Y Description: MonsterX and MonsterY turned into a huge dragon!

4. Twilight Trip* = Surge + UltraDown Effect: Applies TwinHits, Increase, SpeedUp, and Sidestep buffs. Target: All Allies Description: MonsterX and MonsterY turned their skills into a wave of energy! MonsterX/Y/Z became flush with power!

5. White Fog* = SealPray + DanceShut Effect: Inflict SealPray and DanceShut status, ignoring resistances. Target: All Enemies Description: MonsterX and MonsterY loosed a white, obscuring cloud! MonsterX/Y/Z's spells, dances, and breaths are sealed!

6. Rob Vacuum* = RobMagic + RobDance Effect: Drains all MP, ignoring resistances. Target: All Enemies Description: MonsterX and MonsterY created a vaccuum for stealing MP!

7. Counter* = BladeD + Dodge Effect: Applies BladeD buff, returns twice the damage. Target: All Allies Description: MonsterX and MonsterY are looking for a chance!

8. Comical Dance* = PanicAll + PaniDance Effect: Inflicts confusion status, ignoring resistances. Target: All Enemies Description: MonsterX and MonsterY danced in an alluring fashion!

9. Shield of Iron Wall* = Guardian + StrongD Effect: Applies Guardian and StrongD buffs. Target: MonsterX/Y Description: MonsterX and MonsterY are shielding a partner!

10. Enlightenment* = Focus + Meditate Effect: Applies Focus buff and all attacks are criticals next turn. Target: All Allies Description: MonsterX and MonsterY began to concentrate!
