# List of Resistance Multipliers

> Source: McBick "Advanced Skill Guide" FAQ #78461, v1.1 (Neoseeker mirror)
> GameFAQs section URL: https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/gbc/525414-dragon-warrior-monsters-2-cobis-journey/faqs/78461/list-of-resistance-multipliers
> Neoseeker mirror URL: https://www.neoseeker.com/dwm2/faqs/3080761-dragon-quest-monsters-2-cobis-journey-advanced-skill.html (single-page)

> **Still current.** The v4.0 FAQ does not have a standalone resistance-
> multiplier page; instead every skill table embeds the per-resistance values
> in its `Resistance` / `Damage` column (e.g. `0: 70~90 / 1: 59~76 / 2: 35~45 /
> 3: 0`). This file is the canonical summary of what those 0–3 values mean.
> See `skills-physical.md`, `skills-spell.md`, `skills-normal.md`,
> `skills-breath.md`, `skills-dance.md` for the per-skill values.

---

Each monster will have a resistance ranging from 0-3 that determines how effective a skill is against them. There are a total of 27 resistances in this version. Many skills share a resistance. For example, Blaze and FireSlash share the same resistance attribute. However, Blaze is a Spell skill and FireSlash is not. This is important to know because some skills will nullify the actions of a skill based on its type instead of its attribute.

List of Resistance Multipliers;
Resistance of 3 = Damage Multiplier of 0, Success Rate of 0
Resistance of 2 = Damage Multiplier of 0.5, Success Rate of 1/3(33%)
Resistance of 1 = Damage Multiplier of 1, Success Rate of 2/3(66%)
Resistance of 0 = Damage Multiplier of 1.5, Success Rate of 3/3(100%)
