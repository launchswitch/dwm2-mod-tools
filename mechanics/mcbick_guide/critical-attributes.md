## Critical Rates

#### Battle Mechanics

A critical attack can occur for any physical skill or basic attack. There are two critical rates, a monster's natural critical rate and its courage critical rate. Each time a physical attack is used, the game will check both values, giving you two chances to perform a critical. You can check the table below to see what these values are.

Aside from performing a critical, monsters can also perform a strong attack, including skills that aren't physical. In this case, only the motivation stat is checked. This will increase the damage for these skills by about 50%. It is also possible to perform a critical in addition to a strong attack for massive damage.

You can increase your courage stat by choosing the Charge command in battle. As for your motivation stat, this will increase every time you choose the Charge, Mixed, or the Defense Command in battle. The rate at which these skills will increase depends on your monster's level. You will gain 1~3 points per command chosen. Lower level monsters will gain more points per command than higher level monsters.
